# The [Party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) Reforms!
## A Quick Rest
- [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) casts identify on the three artifacts [Adeena](../PCs/Adeena%20Oberon.md) retrieved. [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) learns about the properties of the [Belt of Boundless Strength](../Items/Belt%20of%20Boundless%20Strength.md), [Candle of Invoking the Old Gods](../Items/Candle%20of%20Invoking%20the%20Old%20Gods.md), and the [Necklace of the Vinewyrm](../Items/Necklace%20of%20the%20Vinewyrm.md). [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) shares what he learned to the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md).
- After some discussion, [Thoradin](../PCs/Thoradin%20Goodman.md) decides to attune the [Belt of Boundless Strength](../Items/Belt%20of%20Boundless%20Strength.md). [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md) receives the [Candle of Invoking the Old Gods](../Items/Candle%20of%20Invoking%20the%20Old%20Gods.md).[Adeena](../PCs/Adeena%20Oberon.md) stashes the [Necklace of the Vinewyrm](../Items/Necklace%20of%20the%20Vinewyrm.md).
- [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) and [Noah](../PCs/Noah%20Skie.md) investigate the supposed portal behind the curtain. [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) observes that beyond the curtain is the [Astral Plane](../Locations/Planes/Astral%20Plane.md). [Noah](../PCs/Noah%20Skie.md), on the other hand, feels a force pulling her through the portal.
- The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) decides to open the main entrance to [Miledu](../../Miledu.md)'s prison. [Thoradin](../PCs/Thoradin%20Goodman.md) pushes the door open.
## Chains and Columns
- The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) enter a wide, seemingly empty space. Fog obscures their vision, but they can see the shadow of a large, 100-foot figure standing beyond the glass-like liquid surface. Twelve columns surround them---seven destroyed, while five has chains emanating from them. The chains extend to the gargantuan humanoid figure in front of them.
- Chains from the broken columns lay on the ground. The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) hear clinking sounds from beyond, just obscured by the fog. [Adeena](../PCs/Adeena%20Oberon.md) sees the broken chain being pulled by someone from the fog. She uses divine sense and detects the presence of several fiends.
### A Battle with Devils
- [Noah](../PCs/Noah%20Skie.md) sends the [Driftglobe](../Items/Driftglobe.md) forward, illuminating the area. The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) notices a 25-foot tall figure standing behind the 100-foot humanoid figure. Seven [Bearded Devils](../../Bearded%20Devil.md) wielding chained blades attack the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md).
- During the encounter, [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) feels his [Ymerald Staff](../Items/Mythic%20Items/Ymerald%20Staff.md) channeling magic that he did not cast.
- A [Bearded Devil](../../Bearded%20Devil.md) whispers an incantation, and a [Chained Devil](../../Chained%20Devil.md) appears near the [Driftglobe](../Items/Driftglobe.md). The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) defeats most of the [Bearded Devils](../../Bearded%20Devil.md).
- [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) casts the magic channeling in his [Ymerald Staff](../Items/Mythic%20Items/Ymerald%20Staff.md) on top of the [Chained Devil](../../Chained%20Devil.md). [Arc](../PCs/Arc.md) and twelve [Knights of the Wayraven](../Factions/Knights%20of%20the%20Wayraven.md) emerge from the space, surprising the [Chained Devil](../../Chained%20Devil.md). The devils are defeated.
- [Arc](../PCs/Arc.md) notices that the [Knights of the Wayraven](../Factions/Knights%20of%20the%20Wayraven.md) are in a weakened state.
- [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) casts another spell near him. [Kiris](../PCs/Kiris%20Acquermann.md), [Adikia](../PCs/Adikia%20Unalome.md), [Kristofer](../../Kristofer%20Acquermann.md), [Kirren](../NPCs/Kirren%20Acquermann.md), [Leneia](../NPCs/Leneia%20Yarumcy.md), [Michaela](../NPCs/Michaela%20Randall.md), a wounded [Elix](../../Elix%20Claymore.md), and six [Wayravens](../Factions/Knights%20of%20the%20Wayraven.md) teleport to the empty space.
