# Finale
## Council of the [Gods](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Pantheon%20of%20the%20Old%20Gods.md)
- The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) is transported to a plane where the divine beings dwell. Towering in front of the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) are [The Old Gods](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Pantheon%20of%20the%20Old%20Gods.md). [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) speaks:
> *"Guardians of the mortal world, speak."*
- [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md) asks if they are already dead. [The gods](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Pantheon%20of%20the%20Old%20Gods.md) tell the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) that they are not yet dead. [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md) pleads to send the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) back to the mortal world. [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) tells the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) that [The Old Gods](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Pantheon%20of%20the%20Old%20Gods.md) need the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) too.
- [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) explains the ramifications of killing [Miledu](../../Miledu.md).
> *"[Miledu](../../Miledu.md)'s death will come at a great cost. His death will herald the arrival of an otherworldly evil... We need to know your decision, will you kill [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) or continue binding him?*
- [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) tells the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) that binding [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) will only delay the inevitable, but it might give time for the world to prepare. [The Sun-god](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) gives the ultimate choice to the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md):
> *"The fate of the world is undecided. You have the choice to decide which fate awaits it. Will you bind [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) and buy time for the world, or will you face the great evil head on?"*
- [Thoradin](../PCs/Thoradin%20Goodman.md) asserts that he wants to bind [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) to buy enough time. The others decide that they must defeat [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) once and for all. [Thoradin](../PCs/Thoradin%20Goodman.md) respects the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md)'s decision.
- [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) addresses the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md):
> *"It seems the future is decided. Now I will lend you help."*
### [The New Gods](../Deities/New%20Gods/Pantheon%20of%20the%20Lesser%20Gods.md)
- Four gods appear in front of the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md): [Aulephi](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aulephi.md), [Savras](../Deities/New%20Gods/Savras.md), [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md) and [Macchi](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Macchi.md). The four gods bestow the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) with divine power:
> [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md): *" I will give you healing and rest."*
> 
> [Aulephi](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aulephi.md):  *"I will give you courage."*
> 
> [Macchi](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Macchi.md): *"I will give you my strength."*
> 
> [Savras](../Deities/New%20Gods/Savras.md): *"I will give you foresight."*
- [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md) approaches [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md) and asks her the following:
> *"[Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md), am I on the right path?"*
- [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md) replies:
> *"Child, there is no right path. If your heart desires it, then it is right. There is no direction, no road to walk through, no instruction to follow."*
- [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md) asks again:
> *"Whatever road, [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md), will you walk beside us?"*
- [Aisef](../Deities/New%20Gods/Aisef.md) allays [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md)'s worries.
> *"All this time I have walked beside you."*
- [Akenatun](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Akenatun.md) addresses the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md):
> *"I have given you the freedom to decide your future. Now use our power to defeat your foes."*
- Another flood of divine light envelops the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md). They are soon teleported back to where [Miledu](../../Miledu.md) is.
## Against [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md)
- The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) sees [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md) talking to the bound [Miledu](../../Miledu.md). [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md) hears [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md)'s words:
> *"[Miledu](../../Miledu.md), conduit of Vidra, your power shall be mine."*
- The [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) fights [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md). During the battle, [Macchi](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Macchi.md) tackles [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md), limiting the dragon god's movement.
- After a long-drawn fight, the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md) ultimately defeats [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md).
### [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md)'s Last Words
- [Tiamat](../../Tiamat.md) utters a divine word that banishes half the [party](../Factions/Seven%20Up....md). [Thoradin](../PCs/Thoradin%20Goodman.md), [Kiris](../PCs/Kiris%20Acquermann.md), [Arc](../PCs/Arc.md) and [Adikia](../PCs/Adikia%20Unalome.md) survive the attempt, while [Theobald](../PCs/Theobald%20Clayhollow.md), [Splix](../PCs/Spraugh%20'Splix'%20Calix.md), [Noah](../PCs/Noah%20Skie.md) and [Adeena](../PCs/Adeena%20Oberon.md) are banished in [Suirsya](../Locations/Continents/Suirsya.md).
