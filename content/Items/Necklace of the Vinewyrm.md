## Description
A silver chain with an amber pendant. Encased in the amber is the eye of a large snake.
## Properties
*Wondrous item, rare*

While you wear it, poisons have no effect on you. You are [immune](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Immunity) to the [poisoned](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#PoisonedCondition) condition and have immunity to poison damage.
