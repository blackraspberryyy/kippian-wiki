## Description
A thick and wide leather belt with the symbol of [Macchi](../Deities/Old%20Gods/Macchi.md) on the buckle.
## Properties
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

While wearing this belt, your [Strength](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_12) score changes to 23. If your Strength is already equal to or greater than the belt's score, the item has no effect on you.
